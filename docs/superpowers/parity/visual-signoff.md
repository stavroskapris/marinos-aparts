## Known & Pre-Approved Differences

The following differences between the legacy screenshots and the Astro screenshots are
**expected and intentional** — they do not require remediation.

| # | Scope | Difference | Reason | Status |
|---|-------|-----------|--------|--------|
| 1 | All pages — all viewports | Gallery images rendered in WebP instead of JPEG | Images re-encoded to WebP during Astro migration for performance. Visually equivalent; no quality regression. | **Pre-approved** |
| 2 | Kimon & Irida — lightbox toolbar | Fullscreen button kept; Share button removed | Plan 3 Task 3: share button intentionally dropped (user-approved). Fullscreen parity retained. | **Pre-approved** |
| 3 | Location page — mobile (390px) | Legacy overflowed ~49px horizontally; Astro build shows no overflow | Plan 3 Task 1 fixed the overflow. The Astro version is an improvement, not a regression. | **Pre-approved** |
| 4 | Location page — gr locale | Island names (`Μαύρο Όρος`, `Αγ. Νικόλαος`, `Μουρτεμένο`) quoted with straight `'…'` instead of legacy doubled curly `''…''` | Typographic cleanup — doubled curly quotes were a copy-paste artifact. Documented and allowlisted in `parity-signoff.md` (2026-06-21). | **Pre-approved** |
| 5 | All galleries — lightbox toolbar | Zoom button hidden for images whose intrinsic resolution already fits the viewport (all beach photos ≤1317px; low-res Kimon images e.g. kimon11 1280×960 on large screens). Shows for high-res images (Irida 4608×3456; large Kimon). | Correct PhotoSwipe **v5** behavior — it only shows zoom when the image is actually zoomable. Legacy v4 always rendered the button (a no-op for small images). Source images vary in resolution; forcing zoom would upscale-blur small photos. User-approved 2026-06-21. The real remedy (higher-res source assets) is deferred. | **Pre-approved** |

Any difference **not** listed above and **not** ticked off in the checklist below must be
either fixed or explicitly added here before final sign-off.
